Insurance & payment

Simple billing, no surprises.

We bill most insurance plans directly, so you only pay your portion on the day. No insurance? Ask about our flat-fee visits and 0% payment plans.

  • Direct billing to all major insurers
  • Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP) accepted
  • Alberta Seniors & AISH dental benefits
  • 0% financing on treatment over $1,000
Are you accepting new patients?

Yes! We welcome new patients of all ages, including kids, seniors and families. Most new patients can be seen within a week, and emergencies the same day.

Do you accept the Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP)?

We do. Bring your CDCP member card to your visit and we'll bill Sun Life directly for covered services. We'll tell you about any co-payment before treatment.

What if I'm nervous about the dentist?

You're in good company. Tell us when you book and we'll schedule extra time, walk you through everything first and agree on a "stop" signal. We also offer nitrous oxide and oral sedation.

When should my child first see a dentist?

By their first birthday, or within six months of their first tooth. Visits for kids under three are free and mostly about getting comfortable. Expect lots of high-fives.

Is parking available?

Free parking right outside our door, plus a wheelchair-accessible entrance. We're also two minutes from the 149 Street bus stop.

How much does a visit cost without insurance?

A new patient exam, X-rays and cleaning is a flat $189. For other treatment, we always give you a written estimate before we begin, based on the Alberta Dental Fee Guide.

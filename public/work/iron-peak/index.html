(01) The philosophy

We don't sell memberships. We build athletes. Every program, every coach and every plate on this floor exists for one reason — to take you further than you thought you could go, and keep you there.

Coaching first.
Equipment second.
Ego never.

Iron Peak was founded by two strength coaches who were tired of gyms that were either intimidating warehouses or glossy treadmill farms. We built the place in between — serious equipment, real programming, and coaches who actually know your name.

(08) FAQ

Questions,
answered

Still unsure? Call us at (780) 555-0142 or drop by the front desk — the coffee's on us.

I'm a complete beginner. Is Iron Peak right for me?

Absolutely — about 40% of our members had never touched a barbell before joining. Every new member gets a free assessment and an onboarding plan, and every class has scalable options.

What does the free week pass include?

Seven days of full Summit access: open gym, unlimited classes, the recovery suite and a 30-minute goal session with a coach. No card required.

Can I freeze my membership?

Yes. Freeze for up to 3 months a year for travel, injury or life — free of charge on Summit and Elite, $10/mo on Base Camp.

Is there parking?

Free underground parking for members, plus secure bike storage and an LRT stop two blocks away.

Do you offer corporate or student rates?

We do. Students get 15% off with valid ID, and we partner with 30+ Edmonton companies on corporate wellness. Ask at the front desk.
